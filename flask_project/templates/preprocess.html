Step 1 — Physics Feature Engineering

Derived Physics Feature

Thermal Delta (ΔT)

ΔT = Module Temp − Ambient Temp. Captures photovoltaic thermal saturation stress and cell heating loss (≈ 0.4%/°C power degradation).

TEMP_DIFF = MODULE_TEMPERATURE − AMBIENT_TEMPERATURE

Temporal Features

Diurnal Solar Angle Encoding

HOUR ∈ [0, 23] and MINUTE ∈ [0, 45] encode the sun's elevation angle and diurnal trajectory — the primary drivers of irradiance magnitude.

HOUR = DATE_TIME.hour  ·  MINUTE = DATE_TIME.minute

Daylight Filter

IS_DAY Mask

Only daylight rows (IRRADIATION > 0 or Hour 6–18) are used for regression. Including nocturnal zero-power rows would trivially inflate R² by adding easy-to-predict zeros.

Step 2 — Temporal 80/20 Chronological Holdout

Data is split chronologically — the earliest 80% of daylight records form the training set and the most recent 20% form the sealed holdout. This mimics real-world deployment where the model predicts future power generation it has never seen.

Training Partition 80% Past Chronological training set
Sealed Holdout 20% Future Zero temporal leakage
Sampling Granularity 15 Minutes SCADA synchronized
Scaler Train-only z-score No test leakage in scaling

Why not random shuffle? Shuffling a time series would let the model train on "future" data, inflating performance metrics. Chronological split is the honest evaluation protocol for temporal forecasting.
