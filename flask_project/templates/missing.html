{# ---- Why gaps are hard to standardize ---- #}

Why Gaps Cannot Be Standardized

Variable Gap Length

1 to {{ bundle.gap_summary.max_gap_min }} minutes

Each gap event has a different duration — sensor resets, network timeouts, and power failures create gaps of varying sizes across different days.

Time-of-Day Dependence

Night ≠ Day Gaps

A gap during midday irradiance peak needs different treatment than a nocturnal gap where all power channels are physically zero.

Non-Uniform Distribution

Gaps are sporadic

Missing intervals do not occur at regular offsets — one day may have 2 gaps in the morning, the next day has none, the one after has a 2-hour blackout.
